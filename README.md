## Hackathon Background

This project originated from a women’s safety app concept developed during a hackathon.

Achievements:
- Won 3rd place and received a 100,000 KZT prize
- Attracted real investment interest from entrepreneur Irina Chen, founder of Party Food Taldykorgan, who was prepared to support the project with up to 5 million KZT for further development
- Invited to in-person meetings and discussions regarding product launch and implementation
- Presented the project to the Akim of Taldykorgan and representatives of the city administration

The project reached the stage of real-world discussions and potential launch planning. However, due to the complexity of integrating with police and emergency systems, the final partnership agreement was not completed.
